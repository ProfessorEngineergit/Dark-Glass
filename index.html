Dark GlassBrand idea03
I. BRAND IDEA

Engineered
from light.

Dark Glass is an optical interface material for software that needs to feel precise, dimensional and unmistakably controlled.

It turns depth, blur, edge light and motion into a repeatable system. The atmosphere may feel advanced; the information must remain immediate.

01Precise

Measured spacing, explicit states and legible data.

02Spatial

Depth communicates role instead of decoration.

03Calm

Motion settles softly and never delays access.

04Ownable

One optical logic across distinct products.

Dark Glass speaks directly and calmly. It describes the user’s result before the underlying technology. The language can be curious and ambitious, but never inflated, cryptic or theatrical.

Dark GlassIcons & elements10

Icons & elements

PRODUCT OBJECTS

Product icons are crafted objects with real depth, a controlled light source and one ownable accent. They may differ in color and form while sharing rounded geometry and Obsidian shadow.

INTERFACE GLYPHS

Interface icons use a 1.8px rounded stroke, a 24px grid and minimal detail. Filled geometry is reserved for status, selection and product objects.

Dark GlassField Geometry / Vocabulary21

Shape vocabulary

Every contour carries a role. Geometry may flex with content and state, but its meaning stays stable.

Use expressive forms at moments of intent—not as ambient decoration.

01 / OBSERVE

Orbit

Inspection, identity and spatial focus. Circular because the state has no preferred direction.

02 / CONTINUE

Flow

Navigation, ranges and ongoing processes. A continuous path with calm momentum.

03 / CREATE

Bloom

Primary creation and transformation. It opens toward the action as intent increases.

04 / GROUP

Dock

Related tools and attached actions. The asymmetric corners expose the grouping direction.

05 / SIGNAL

Beacon

Notifications, direction and time-sensitive attention. The tapered edge points to consequence.

Dark GlassField Geometry / Grammar22

Mass & charge

Mass is visual gravity: size, blur, shadow and thickness express importance.

Charge is live intent: color, edge light and asymmetry express state.

MASS / HIERARCHY
Quiet16 blur Base24 blur Focus40 blur

Thicker material belongs to fewer, more consequential controls. Never raise every object at once.

CHARGE / STATE
0Rest 1Intent 2Valid 3Alert

Color never works alone: each charged state also changes edge, shadow or contour.
