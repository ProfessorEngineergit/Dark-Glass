01 / System

Not glassmorphism.
A material with rules.

Decoration becomes a brand only when it is repeatable. Dark Glass defines the geometry, optical behavior, hierarchy and motion shared by every interface in the system.

01

Depth communicates role

Quiet surfaces organize. Raised surfaces invite. Luminous surfaces focus. Blur is hierarchy, never wallpaper.

02

Light follows intent

Edges brighten exactly where the pointer approaches. The material acknowledges attention before a click.

03

Precision remains visible

Measured spacing, restrained radii and monospaced metadata keep expressive visuals credible.

04

Motion proves physics

Elements accelerate quickly and settle softly. Nothing glitches, blocks or performs without purpose.

02 / Material

Five depths.
One optical logic.

Each level changes fill, saturation, blur and edge intensity together. That keeps a dense engineering interface legible without turning it into a wall of rectangles.
